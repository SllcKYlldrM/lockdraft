## About LockDraft

**LockDraft** is a technology blog focused on practical, no-hype coverage of artificial intelligence: model and tool announcements, agent architectures, automation workflows, and a growing library of ready-to-use prompts.

### What you'll find here

- **AI News** — model releases, tool launches, and what they actually mean for builders.
- **Agents** — patterns for building AI agents: tool use, planning, memory, multi-agent systems, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP).
- **Automation** — practical workflows built with tools like n8n, Make, and plain scripts.
- **Tutorials** — step-by-step guides for getting things running.
- **Prompts** — a curated, filterable library of prompts you can copy and adapt.

### Who this is for

Builders, tinkerers, and anyone who wants to actually *use* AI rather than just read hot takes about it.
